# Phase 4 — Kinematics & Motion

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-planned-5A6B82)
![Skills](https://img.shields.io/badge/skills-Kinematics_·_Control_·_tf2-16243B)

> Give the platform parts that move and articulate — precisely and predictably.

## Goal

Introduce controlled motion — components that move, orient, and position themselves accurately — and the math and control theory that make that motion reliable.

## What it will demonstrate

- **Coordinate frames and transforms** (ROS2 `tf2`)
- **Forward and inverse kinematics**
- **Feedback control** — PID, control loops, stability
- **Motion planning** and precise positioning of articulated components

## Key concept

Motion is where robotics becomes physical. Correct geometry and control are what separate smooth, dependable movement from unpredictable behavior — and this phase connects directly to control-systems fundamentals.

## Tech stack

`C++` · `Python` · `ROS2` · `tf2` · `kinematics` · `PID control`

## Status

**Planned.** Builds on the ROS2 foundation from Phase 3.
